Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Rouse is an alarm clock. It is built to work on your phone, without an account, and to keep what happens in your bedroom on your phone. This policy explains exactly what data Rouse handles and why.

What stays on your phone

Photos and video taken for missions (Object Hunt, Sky Snap, Make Your Bed, Touch Grass, Scan an Item, Push-ups, Squats) are checked on your phone by the system's on-device image tools and deleted right after the check. They are never uploaded or saved.

Your voice in Read Aloud and Morning Verse is turned into text by your phone's on-device speech recognition. Audio is not uploaded or stored.

Motion data for Shake and Walk It Off is read from your phone's sensors while the mission is open and is not stored.

Sounds you record for your own alarms are saved on your phone only and are deleted when you remove them or delete the app.

Your alarms, wake-up history, streaks, badges and settings are stored on your phone.

What we store online, and only if you choose

Rouse gives every install an anonymous ID (Firebase Authentication) so purchases and an optional backup can be linked to you without asking for any personal details.

Push notifications: Rouse uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver occasional messages. It gives this install a random device token and sends basic delivery diagnostics to Google. The token isn't linked to your email or account. Messages only appear if you allow notifications, and you can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

If you choose Save my progress, we store your email address (to send you a sign-in link) and a backup of your alarms, wake-up history and settings in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). This is used only to restore your data on a new phone.

You can delete your account and this backup at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether your Rouse Pro subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your anonymous ID and your purchase receipts from the store.

What we don't do

No advertising and no ad tracking.

No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We rely on the anonymous, aggregated statistics Apple and Google provide to every developer.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone except the service providers named above, who process it only to provide their service to Rouse.

Permissions

Alarms and notifications: to ring your alarms and send the wake-up check.

Camera, microphone and speech recognition: only during the missions that need them, and the microphone when you record your own alarm sound.

Motion & fitness: only for step-counting missions.

Every permission is optional. If you decline one, missions that need it offer a backup mission instead.

Children

Rouse is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can see, export or delete the data we hold about you by contacting us. Deleting your account in the app removes your backup and sign-in immediately.

If you live in the EU/UK or California, you have additional rights under the GDPR or CCPA, including access, correction and deletion. Contact us to use them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change is significant.

Rouse is published by the developer shown on our App Store and Google Play pages, who is responsible for your data under this policy.

For questions, contact us through the support address on our App Store or Google Play listing.
