Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Waykin is an alarm clock. It is built to work on your phone, without an account, and to keep what happens in your bedroom on your phone. This policy explains exactly what data Waykin handles and why.

What stays on your phone

Photos and video taken for missions (Object Hunt, Sky Snap, Make Your Bed, Touch Grass, Scan an Item, Push-ups, Squats) are checked on your phone by the system's on-device image tools and deleted right after the check. They are never uploaded or saved.

Your voice in Read Aloud and Morning Verse is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition. Waykin asks for on-device recognition; on phones that don't support it, the audio is processed by Apple's or Google's speech service under their privacy policies. Waykin never receives or stores your audio.

Motion data for Shake and Walk It Off is read from your phone's sensors while the mission is open and is not stored.

Sounds you record or pick from your files for your own alarms are saved on your phone only. They are not backed up, and they are deleted when you remove them or delete the app.

The name you enter during setup, your alarms, wake-up history, streaks, badges, Night mode times and settings are stored on your phone.

What we store online

Waykin gives every install an anonymous ID (Google Firebase Authentication) so purchases and an optional backup can be linked to you without asking for any personal details.

Push notifications: Waykin uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver occasional messages. It gives this install a random device token and sends basic delivery diagnostics to Google. The token isn't linked to your email or account. Messages only appear if you allow notifications, and you can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings. Your alarms never depend on this.

If you create an account, your email address and password are handled by Google Firebase Authentication (the password is stored only in hashed form, never readable by us), and a backup is kept in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). The backup holds your alarms (times, labels, missions, and the names and barcodes of items you register for Scan an Item), your wake-up history and your settings. It is used only to sign you in and restore your data on a new phone.

Only you can read your backup: it is protected by your sign-in and sent over encrypted connections.

Google and RevenueCat may process this data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under their own security and privacy commitments.

Purchases

Subscriptions and free trials are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether your Waykin Pro subscription or trial is active. RevenueCat receives your anonymous ID, your purchase receipts from the store and basic device and app information needed to validate them.

What we don't do

No advertising and no ad tracking. Waykin doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We rely on the anonymous, aggregated statistics Apple and Google provide to every developer.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone except the service providers named above, who process it only to provide their service to Waykin.

Permissions

Notifications: to ring your alarms (on iPhone, alarms ring through notifications that repeat until you finish your mission), and to send the wake-up check and the reminders you turn on.

Camera, microphone and speech recognition: only during the missions that need them, and the microphone when you record your own alarm sound.

Motion & fitness: only for the shake and step-counting missions.

Android only: "Alarms & reminders", full-screen alerts and an exception from battery optimisation, so alarms ring on time and show over the lock screen.

Every permission is optional. If you decline one, missions that need it offer a backup mission instead.

How long we keep data

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app.

Your account and backup are kept until you delete them. Deleting your account removes your email, sign-in and backup straight away.

Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Deleting your data

Settings → Account → Delete my data deletes your alarms, history, settings and recordings on this phone, and your online backup. Your account stays.

Settings → Account → Delete account deletes your account and backup. You can also ask us by email (see our Delete account page).

Deleting data or your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account.

Children

Waykin is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

You can see, export, correct or delete the data we hold about you by contacting us. Deleting your account in the app removes your backup and sign-in immediately.

If you live in the EU/UK, California or Australia, you have rights under the GDPR, the CCPA or the Australian Privacy Act, including access, correction and deletion. Contact us to use them. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Australia, the OAIC).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change is significant.
