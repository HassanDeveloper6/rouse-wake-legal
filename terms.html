The app

Waykin is an alarm clock that asks you to finish a short mission to turn an alarm off. It is a tool to help you wake up; it does not guarantee that you will.

Alarms depend on your phone: its volume, battery, Silent mode, Focus or Do Not Disturb settings, operating-system restrictions and the permissions you grant. On iPhone, alarms ring through notifications, which iOS mutes in Silent mode and in Focus unless you allow Waykin; while Waykin is open or in the background, it plays the alarm at your media volume. Please keep your phone charged and your volume up, and check the Alarm reliability guide in Settings. Do not rely on Waykin as your only alarm where missing it could cause harm.

Missions and safety

Some missions ask you to move (push-ups, squats, walking). Only do them if you're able to, and stop if you feel unwell. Every mission has a backup option.

Photo and voice checks run on your phone and can make mistakes. After three failed photo checks, Waykin offers a backup mission.

Waykin Pro

Waykin Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription, billed weekly or monthly, that unlocks every mission, sound pack and Pro feature. Prices are shown in the app before you buy, in your local currency.

Payment is charged to your App Store or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically at the same price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel them in your store account settings.

Free trial: if your plan includes one (for example, 7 days free on the monthly plan for new subscribers), you won't be charged during the trial. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the trial turns into a paid subscription and you're charged the plan price. Each account can use a trial only once, and any unused part of a trial ends when you buy a subscription.

When a trial or subscription ends, your alarms keep ringing on the free plan; Pro-only missions and sounds switch to free ones.

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.
