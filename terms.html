The app

Rouse is an alarm clock that asks you to finish a short mission to turn an alarm off. It is a tool to help you wake up; it does not guarantee that you will.

Alarms depend on your phone: its volume, battery, Focus or Do Not Disturb settings, operating-system restrictions and permissions you grant. Please keep your phone charged and check the Alarm reliability guide in Settings. Do not rely on Rouse as your only alarm where missing it could cause harm.

Missions and safety

Some missions ask you to move (push-ups, squats, walking). Only do them if you're able to, and stop if you feel unwell. Every mission has a backup option.

Photo and voice checks run on your phone and can make mistakes. After three failed photo checks, Rouse offers a backup mission.
