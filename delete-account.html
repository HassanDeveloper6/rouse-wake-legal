Delete your Rouse account

This page explains how to delete the account you created with Save my progress in Rouse, and what happens to your data.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Rouse and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Can’t open the app?

Write to the support address on our App Store or Google Play listing, from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and sign-in.
  • Your online backup: alarms, wake-up history and settings.
  • The anonymous ID that links your purchases to the account.

What is kept

  • Alarms, history and recordings stored on your phone stay there until you delete the app.
  • Purchase records kept by Apple or Google, and by our purchase provider RevenueCat, as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Rouse Pro subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account (see Support).
