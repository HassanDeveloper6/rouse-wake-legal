Common questions

Will my alarm ring if Waykin is closed?

Yes. On iPhone with iOS 26 or later, Waykin uses Apple's system alarms, which ring even in Silent mode and when the app is closed. On older iPhones, keep Night mode on at bedtime and the ringer on. On Android, allow "Alarms & reminders" and turn off battery optimisation. Settings → Alarm reliability walks you through it.

I pressed Stop on the lock screen. Why is it ringing again?

That's on purpose. The alarm only stops for good when you finish your mission. Tap "Start mission" instead.

The photo check keeps failing.

Turn on a light, get closer so the object fills the frame, and hold still. After three tries you can switch to a Math mission instead.

What is the wake-up check?

A few minutes after your mission, Waykin asks "Still awake?". Tap within 60 seconds, or the alarm rings again. You can change the delay or turn it off per alarm.

How do I restore Waykin Pro on a new phone?

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore. It works with the same Apple ID or Google account, no Waykin account needed.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on iPhone, or Play Store → Payments & subscriptions on Android.

Subscriptions and refunds

Waykin Pro is billed by Apple or Google. To cancel or change a plan:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions, or play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Got a new phone? Open the Waykin paywall or Settings → Account and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play under their policies.
